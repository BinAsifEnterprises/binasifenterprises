06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions, answered honestly.

The things clients ask us most before starting a project — answered plainly, with no sales talk. Anything else on your mind, just ask us on WhatsApp.

What services do you offer?

We offer five core services: content creation, social media management, AI content creation, graphic design and digital marketing. You can book a single service — such as a logo or a batch of reels — or combine several into one connected plan, depending on what your brand actually needs.

How does pricing work?

We don't publish fixed packages, because no two brands need exactly the same work. Tell us what you want to achieve and we will prepare a clear quote for that scope before anything begins — you approve the work and the price first, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope. A single design asset is usually a matter of days, a batch of video content takes longer to plan and produce, and social media management runs as an ongoing monthly rhythm. Before we start, we agree a realistic timeline with you — and if anything changes, we tell you early.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes revision rounds, agreed with you as part of the scope before work begins. We refine the work within the agreed creative direction until it is right. If you later want to change direction entirely, we will discuss openly what that means for the scope before doing anything extra.

How do we get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or send an email — or use the brief builder above to outline what you need. We have a short conversation about your goals, then share a scope, timeline and quote. Once you approve it, work begins. No long forms and no pressure.

Which platforms do you create for?

We create and manage content for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and X. If your audience lives mainly on one platform, we focus there; if you are building across several, each piece of content is adapted to fit the platform it appears on rather than copied across.
